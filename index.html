🛍️ National Small Business Week — May 4–10

Three Things You Can Do This Week

Every business we cover on this show — every restaurant, every shop, every service provider — is somebody's livelihood and somebody's risk. This week is the week to do something about it.

✅ Pick at least one — all of them take 60 seconds or less

1. Spend a dollar somewhere local you wouldn't normally go — a new lunch spot, a small retail shop, a service business. Gift cards count.
2. Write a Google review for a small business you already love — Google reviews are still the #1 thing that helps small businesses show up when somebody searches for them.
3. Tag the business when you visit — Facebook, Instagram, wherever you post. Social proof from a neighbor beats any ad they could buy.
