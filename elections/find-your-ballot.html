A few quick reminders for May 19

Voter registration is closed.

The deadline was April 20, 2026. If you weren't already registered, you can't vote on May 19. (You can still register now for the November general election — go to vrsws.sos.ky.gov/ovrweb.)

Mail-in absentee request is closed.

The portal closed May 5. If you didn't request a mail ballot already, vote in person.

Vote-center model.

Hardin County uses a vote-center system in 2026. Any registered voter can use any of 15 election-day locations from 6 AM to 6 PM on May 19.

Three days of early voting.

No-excuse early voting opens Thursday May 14, Friday May 15, and Saturday May 16 — 8 AM to 4:30 PM at the Clerk's office and Colvin Community Center.
